Frostbeast Slopes

Ski down the mountain. Dodge trees and rocks, launch off ramps and spin in the air for style points. Past 2,000 m the Frostbeast wakes up and chases you. Use turbo and the trees to get away.

iPad

  • Hold your finger where you want to ski
  • JUMP to hop, tap again in the air to spin
  • Hold TURBO to go faster

Keyboard

  • ← → turn, ↓ straight down
  • Space hop / spin
  • F turbo, P pause
